05 // ABOUT

About Me

I'm Diego, a gameplay programmer focused on Unreal Engine 5 and C++.

I come from a Computer Science background and spent four years at Amazon Logistics as a Process Specialist and Data Analyst: auditing systems, finding where processes broke and fixing them with data. That mindset carries straight into game development — debugging, iterating and building systems that hold up under pressure.

After the programming track at Event Horizon School of Game Development, I've been working as an independent developer since 2024: shipping game jam titles, building gameplay systems in C++ and Blueprints, and experimenting with AI, multiplayer and procedural mechanics.

I'm looking for a role as a Gameplay Programmer, and I'm also open to Tools Developer positions.
